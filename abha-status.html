ABHA is a secure digital health identity that helps you access and share your health records digitally under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission.

What is ABHA?

ABHA stands for Ayushman Bharat Health Account. It gives you a unique 14-digit health ID that connects your medical history safely across hospitals, clinics, and emergency responders.
