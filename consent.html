Before you continue

PulseTag uses a QR code to make your emergency information available when you need help.

1
What becomes visible? Your emergency profile can include your blood group, allergies, medications, and emergency contact details.
2
Who can see it? Anyone who scans your PulseTag QR code can view the emergency information you have chosen to share.
3
You stay in control Only the information in your emergency profile is intended for QR-based emergency access.
Please agree only if you are comfortable making your emergency profile available through your PulseTag QR code.

What happens if I don't agree?

Your emergency information will remain saved in PulseTag, but QR-based emergency sharing will not be enabled. You can come back and give consent later.
